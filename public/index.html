We shoot weddings the way you'll want to remember them

A wedding is nine hours of chaos wearing silk. Somewhere inside it your father is adjusting his watch for the fourth time, your cousins are arguing about the seating, and your grandmother has quietly decided she is in charge of the flowers.

That's the film. The portraits matter — we'll make you look extraordinary in them — but the reason you'll open the album in twenty years is the half-second before someone realised they were being photographed.
